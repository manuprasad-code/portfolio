Problem & Constraints

A vehicle capable of inspecting at shallow depths, targeted to carry varying types of payloads. The architecture had to be modular enough to adjust thruster configuration and swap between a higher or lower size hull.

Key Engineering Challenges

Three problems defined the design: keeping water out at depth, keeping the vehicle balanced, and keeping the electronics cool inside a sealed hull.

1

Sealing for 300 m depth

Design and manufacturing of pressure penetrators and the sealing stack that holds at full depth rating.

Seal stack / penetrator detail
2

CB & CG management

Balancing centre of buoyancy against centre of gravity across every payload configuration to keep the vehicle stable and trimmed.

CB / CG study
3

Thermal management

Moving heat out of a fully sealed hull and into the surrounding water without compromising the pressure boundary.
